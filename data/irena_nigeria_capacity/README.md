## Comparison with the IEA, 2023

IRENA puts total generation at 37,402 GWh against the IEA's 40,958 GWh (9.5% lower); gas
generation at 27,599 GWh against 31,640 GWh. The prototype uses the IEA figures and uses IRENA as
a cross-check. IRENA lists the IEA among its contributors, so agreement between the two is not
fully independent.

The three files are internally consistent: the renewable share of 2023 generation computed from
the generation file (26.09%) matches the share file.

## Note

Off-grid capacity (about 129 against 14,293 on-grid in 2023) is mainly solar and mini-grids. It
does **not** represent Nigeria's private diesel and petrol generator fleet, which is not captured
here.
