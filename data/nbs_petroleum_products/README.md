## Use

Bounds the fuel available to Nigeria's back-up generators. Petrol plus diesel alone is 98.5% of
the IEA's total oil-products final consumption (899,924 TJ in 2023). Truck-out measures
distribution, including fuel that leaves the country informally, so it is an upper bound on
domestic use.
