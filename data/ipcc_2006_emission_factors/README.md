# IPCC 2006 default CO₂ emission factors for fuel combustion

- **Source:** *2006 IPCC Guidelines for National Greenhouse Gas Inventories*, Volume 2 (Energy),
  Chapter 1, **Table 1.4** "Default Emission Factors for Stationary Combustion" (printed p. 1.23).
- **URL:** https://www.ipcc-nggip.iges.or.jp/public/2006gl/pdf/2_Volume2/
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-21.
- **Licence:** check IPCC/IGES terms before reuse.

Nigeria's national greenhouse-gas inventory also uses the 2006 IPCC Guidelines and IPCC default
factors for energy.

## Files

- `IPCC_2006_Vol2_Ch1_Introduction.pdf` — the chapter, unmodified.
- `default_co2_factors_table_1_4.csv` — the rows relevant to Nigeria's fuel mix.

## Factors, kg CO₂ per TJ (net calorific value)

| Fuel | Default | 95% lower | 95% upper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural gas | 56,100 | 54,300 | 58,300 |
| Liquefied petroleum gases | 63,100 | 61,600 | 65,600 |
| Motor gasoline | 69,300 | 67,500 | 73,000 |
| Other kerosene | 71,900 | 70,800 | 73,700 |
| Crude oil | 73,300 | 71,100 | 75,500 |
| Gas/diesel oil | 74,100 | 72,600 | 74,800 |
| Residual fuel oil | 77,400 | 75,500 | 78,800 |
| Other bituminous coal | 94,600 | 89,500 | 99,700 |
| Wood / wood waste | 112,000 | 95,000 | 132,000 |
| Charcoal | 112,000 | 95,000 | 132,000 |

## Biomass

Under the Guidelines, CO₂ from burning biomass is **not included in national energy totals**; it is
reported as an information item and accounted for in land use (AFOLU). Energy-sector CO₂ in the
prototype therefore excludes biomass.
