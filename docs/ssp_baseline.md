# SSP Baseline (`src/ssp_baseline`)

## Overview

Builds, per SSP marker and per year from `time_horizon.base_year` to `end_year`, the
prototype's no-policy baseline: economy-wide fossil-and-industrial CO₂, electrification, the
low-emission share of electricity and its composition, population, and the clean-cooking income
index. The method, its denominator decision and what it deliberately does not carry are in the
module docstring of `builder.py`.

## Data Inputs

- IMAGE 3.2 SSP2021, Africa R10 (`inputs.image_dir`): electricity by source, final energy,
  GDP|PPP, population.
- IIASA SSP export for Nigeria (`inputs.iiasa`): population and GDP|PPP by SSP narrative.
- IEA 2023 current-release tables (`inputs.iea.*_current`): the anchors are cross-checked
  against them on every run; a mismatch or a missing table is an error. Grid generation is
  read by source: `inputs.iea_generation_lines` names the IEA line of each IAMC source, and a
  source without a line must be an observed zero in `ssp_baseline.anchors.generation_gwh`.

## Key Equations

- Total final energy: `TFE(t) = TFE_base × (P_t/P_base) × exp(b L + q L²) × T(t, base_year)`,
  `L = ln(y_t / y_base)`, with `b = ssp_baseline.demand.elasticity` and
  `q = ssp_baseline.demand.elasticity_quadratic` (Burke & Csereklyei 2016, Table 5; transferred).
- Emissions: `M(t) = M_anchor × (P_t/P_anchor) × exp(b L + q L²) × T(t, anchor_year)`,
  `L = ln(y_t / y_anchor)`, with `M_anchor = ssp_baseline.anchors.emissions_mt` in
  `time_horizon.anchor_year`.
- R10 efficiency residual: `T(y, ref) = [FE(y)/FE(ref)] / [P(y)/P(ref) × exp(b L + q L²)]`,
  all Africa R10, so the income effect is not counted twice.
- Electrification share (generation / final energy, since 2026-10-22):
  `s(t) = s_base × exp(bs L + qs L²) × T_el(t, base_year)`, `L = ln(y_t / y_base)`. The share
  law is the electricity law less the final-energy law, `bs = be − b` and `qs = qe − q` (0.30 and
  −0.09), with `be = ssp_baseline.demand.electricity_elasticity` and
  `qe = ssp_baseline.demand.electricity_elasticity_quadratic` (Burke & Csereklyei 2016, Table 6
  col. 6; transferred). Electricity = share × final energy then follows the electricity law.
- R10 electrification residual: `T_el(y, ref) = [s(y)/s(ref)] / exp(bs L + qs L²)`, all Africa
  R10, with `s = Secondary Energy|Electricity / Final Energy`. Until 2026-10-22 the share took
  R10's index `s(y)/s(base)` as it stood, which carried R10's faster income growth.
  - Income is counted once only as the transferred law measures it. The law explains a quarter
    to two-fifths of R10's share growth to 2050, so `T_el` carries most of it, including any
    income response IMAGE has beyond the law.
  - The law peaks at `L` = 1.67 and is past the paper's 75th percentile at `L` = 2.0, where the
    paper stops reporting elasticities. Once R10's income passes the peak, removing its income
    growth raises `T_el`, and by 2100 the shares exceed the raw index in all markers but
    SSP3-7.0.
  - The script prints each share's three factors, the law's part of R10's share growth and the
    year each income path passes `L` = 2.0, and logs a warning for each such path. Sizes and
    gaps: `config.yaml`, `ssp_baseline.demand.electricity_elasticity`.
- Fossil split: back-up generators' share of fossil generation `k = k0 × e0/e`.
- Clean-cooking index: `(income per head_t / income per head_base) ** cooking_elasticity`.

## Configuration (`config.yaml`)

`ssp_baseline.anchors` (base-year generation by source, back-up generators, final energy,
emissions), `ssp_baseline.demand`, `ssp_baseline.envelope`,
`ssp_baseline.cumulative_start_year` (start of BLOCK 1's `cum`, the prototype's 2025–2100
window) and `ssp_baseline.diagnostics_year` (stdout only), plus `scenarios`, `time_horizon`,
`inputs` and `parameters.burke_csereklyei_2016.table5.total` (its 25th and 75th percentiles
set the income span the electrification warning uses). Each value carries its source and gap
notes in the file.

## Outputs

`ssp_baseline.output_block` (`nigeria_baseline_block.js`, four blocks) and, with
`--update-prototype`, the same blocks spliced into `ssp_baseline.prototype_html`.
