Layers 1–6 · how much detail you see

How it works

The step bar at the top shows six steps: three choices, then three sets of results. Within each step the same content is stacked in layers, shallowest first. Nothing below the surface is hidden — it is folded away until you ask for it.

  • 1The answer. The headline figure, the chart or the map.
  • 2What it means, in plain words. The caption beside every figure and the label on every lever: unit, year, and what is compared with what.
  • 3A term or range where you meet it. Hover or keyboard focus gives a one-sentence definition or the model range. Nothing navigates away.
  • 4What could make this higher or lower. The fold on every results screen — the one layer put in front of you rather than waiting to be asked.
  • 5The full explanation. The four numbered entry points on the opening screen and the folds beside each chart.
  • 6The numbers, and the sources. "Show data table" under each chart, and the papers behind each step.
