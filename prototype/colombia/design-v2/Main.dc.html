Welcome to the climate-economic policy design tool

Explore climate-economic pathways for your country. You will make assumptions and the model will show the possible outcomes.

Transparent models— published methods, settings stated

Some parts are published methods, run with the settings stated in the tool: the SSP scenario database, the FaIR climate model, and a social cost of carbon found by adding one tonne of CO₂ in 2025 and tracing the damage it causes, with the damage function and discounting stated. Other parts are labelled prototype constructions — the Colombian emissions baseline and the air-pollution response — not a Colombia model run.

Open data— public statistics and scenario databases

The inputs are public statistics and published datasets: UPME's Colombian energy balance, the Global Carbon Budget, Ember, the World Bank (development indicators and the CCKP climate portal), the WHO, McDuffie et al. surface PM₂.₅, the EMEP/EEA emission-factor guidebook and the IIASA SSP database. Licences differ by source and have not all been checked.
