Target for fossil-fuel and industrial carbon dioxide emissions in 2050, expressed as a change from Colombia's 2024 level. Land use is not part of this target.

● Observed 2024: 92.7 Mt CO₂ (Global Carbon Budget)

What has Colombia already committed to?

Colombia's NDC sets two absolute limits on net national emissions of all six greenhouse gases, including agriculture, forestry and other land use:

  • 2030At most 169.44 Mt CO₂eq — ratified from NDC 2.0 (2020).
  • 2035At most 155 to 161 Mt CO₂eq — new in NDC 3.0.

Not on this slider's basis. The slider covers fossil-fuel and industrial CO₂ only, in 2050. The NDC covers all gases and land use, in 2030 and 2035. Gas coverage, sector coverage and target year all differ, so neither figure can be read off the other.

Source: NDC 3.0 — Transformaciones para la Vida (República de Colombia), Table 1, p. 54. UNFCCC NDC registry ↗

Where this figure comes from ◆ Derived from a sourced series

The archived 2024 observation is 92.6594 Mt, rounded to 92.7 Mt in the label. It marks the 2024 level on the slider; the 2050 target is a percentage change from it. The marker is historical context, not a 2050 SSP baseline or a recommended target.

Source: Global Carbon Budget via Our World in Data, archived annual Colombia fossil-fuel and industrial CO₂ series, 2024.

Electrification of final energy

Share of all energy used as electricity · today 17.8%
{{t.label}}
{{elec.disp}}of final energy in 2050
About this lever

Share of energy used by households, transport, services and industry that is supplied as electricity in 2050. The top of the range is an arithmetic bound, not a pathway: industrial process heat, feedstocks, aviation and shipping cannot all be electrified.

● Colombia 2023: 17.8% of final energy (UPME energy balance)

Where this figure comes from ✓ Sourced

Grid electricity plus self- and co-generation, 271,541 TJ, divided by total final energy consumption, 1,528,901 TJ, giving 17.76%. This is electricity as a share of all final energy, which is lower than figures usually quoted for electricity access or for household energy.

Source: UPME, Balance Energético Colombiano (BECO) 2023.

Share of electricity generated from renewable and other low-emission sources in 2050.

● Colombia 2023: 76.4% of electricity (UPME energy balance)

Where this figure comes from ✓ Sourced

Hydro, wind and solar together supply 220,470 TJ of 288,418 TJ of electricity products, giving 76.44%. Hydro dominates this share, so it moves with rainfall: a dry year lowers it without any change in installed capacity.

Source: UPME, Balance Energético Colombiano (BECO) 2023.
