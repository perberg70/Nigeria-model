Scope
The ODbL notice covers department-boundaries.json and its boundary geometry
embedded in colombia-prototype.html and design-v2/RegionMap.dc.html.
It does not relicense the application code, thesis text or independently
sourced climate/health data.

Adaptations and complete data offer
The complete adapted boundary dataset used for these maps is provided free
of charge, without registration or additional restrictions, in the adjacent
department-boundaries.json file. It contains all 32 displayed mainland
department/district paths, their names, regional grouping and SVG viewBox.
The same file is distributed alongside the deployed prototype and linked
from each department map.

The canvas simplified and rounded the geometry, omitted small polygons,
normalised some names and grouped departments into five natural regions.
San Andrés is represented by a labelled schematic circle, not an island
boundary; its display coordinates are also included in the JSON.
SVG coordinates are display coordinates, not longitude and latitude.
The original one-off simplification script was not archived. Providing the
entire adapted dataset is the chosen access route under ODbL section 4.6(a);
this offer does not depend on reproducing the original transformation.

Reuse
Keep the attribution and licence notices when redistributing this dataset.
Publicly shared adaptations of the dataset must follow ODbL's share-alike
and data-access terms. Consult the licence linked above for the full terms.
We treat the adapted boundary collection as a Derivative Database and meet
those obligations instead of relying on an exemption for rendered maps.
